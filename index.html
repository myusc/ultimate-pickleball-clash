Rules and controls
Serve
Underhand and crosscourt, past the kitchen. Space or any shot button. Hold left or right to aim.
2 bounces
The serve and the return must each bounce before anyone volleys.
Kitchen
No volleys while standing in the kitchen. Let the ball bounce first.
Mode
Classic: only you use an ultimate. Ultimate: all four players use theirs.
Scoring
Only the serving team scores. Both partners serve before a side out. The call reads server score, receiver score, server number.
Drive
Space or J. Becomes a smash on a high ball near the net.
Dink
K. Soft, into their kitchen.
Lob
L. High over their heads.
Ultimate
M or ULT. Fills as you hit and win points. Every player has their own ultimate.
Move
W A S D or arrows. On a phone, drag on the left side.
